Supply schedule for this week

Mains flushing starts on Monday in wards 12 to 18. Expect lower pressure between six and ten in the morning while crews open hydrants along the main roads. Store a bucket of water the night before if you need it early.

What changes

Residents with medical equipment that needs running water can call the priority line to be told before the supply drops in their street. Calls are answered from seven in the morning until eight at night on weekdays.

Tankers will stand at the ward offices from nine until five on each flushing day. Bring your own containers, at most forty litres per household per visit.

Pressure after the work

Pressure returns to normal within two hours of crews leaving a street. If your water runs brown, let the cold tap run for three minutes before using it for drinking or cooking. Brown water is sediment stirred up by the flushing and clears on its own.

Questions

Billing questions go to the billing desk, and outages can be reported through the help line at any hour.

Schedules may change at short notice because of weather or emergency repairs.
