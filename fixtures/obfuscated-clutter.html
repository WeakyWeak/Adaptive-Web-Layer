New opening hours from 3 November

By the Library Services team · 4 min read

What changes

From Monday 3 November the main branch opens at 9:30 instead of 9:00 on weekdays, and stays open until 20:00 on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Saturday hours do not change: 10:00 to 16:00. The branch is closed on Sundays, as before.

The children's library on the first floor keeps its own hours. It opens at 10:00 every day except Monday, when it is closed for story-time preparation and cleaning.

Why

Visitor counts show that fewer than 40 people use the library between 9:00 and 9:30, while the evening study rooms are full on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Moving half an hour from the morning to two late evenings gives students more time without adding staff costs.

Returning books

The return box by the front door stays open all night. Books returned after closing time are checked in the next morning and no late fee is charged for the night they spent in the box. Do not leave books on the steps: they are not insured there.

Getting help

Staff at the information desk can renew loans, reserve study rooms and help you join the library. You can also renew up to five times online, unless another reader has reserved the book.
